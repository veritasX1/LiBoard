For everyone coming from the iPhone

People coming from the iPhone often miss the keyboard most. With LiBoard, there is nothing to relearn.

Keyboard

The familiar layout

Letters, numbers and symbols are where you know them from the iPhone. At the bottom always the same row: 123 · Emoji · space · action.

Trackpad

The space bar
becomes a trackpad.

Hold down the space bar. The keys fade, and your finger moves the cursor through the text, across lines too.

  • Across the whole keyboard, not just the space bar.
  • Letting go ends the mode without typing a space.
  • A short swipe on the space bar moves the cursor as usual.

Features

More Features

No System Bar

There are no Android buttons below the keyboard. “Done” at the top right hides it, as on the iPhone.

Superscript and subscript

On request, the suggestion bar shows switches for x² and x₂, for H₂O or m² for example. Turn them on in the settings under “Raised & lowered characters”.

German and English

Both languages at once, without switching. Suggestions come from both dictionaries.

Emoji with search

All categories sit in one strip. Long-press for skin tones, and search finds emoji by word. You can set your own emoji font.

Typing test

Measures error rate, corrections and speed so you can compare LiBoard with your previous keyboard. The results stay on the phone.

Settings like iOS

Grouped lists and large titles as in the iPhone’s settings, designed following Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines.

Privacy

No Internet Access

A keyboard sees everything you type, passwords included. That is why LiBoard lacks the permission for the internet, and none of it can leave your phone.

Origin

Built on
HeliBoard.

LiBoard is a fork of HeliBoard, the free keyboard by Helium314 and many contributors. As there, the typing recognition comes from the Android source code. LiBoard changes the look and handling, removes the optional data collection and remains free software under the GPL-3.0.
