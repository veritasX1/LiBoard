LiBoard brings the feel of the iPhone keyboard to Android: calm keys, clear special keys and many variants on long press.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Look based on Apple's HIG, light and dark</li>
<li>123 and #+= pages like on the iPhone</li>
<li>Long-press the space bar to move the cursor like a trackpad</li>
<li>“Done” in the suggestion bar hides the keyboard</li>
<li>German and English at the same time, without switching</li>
<li>Auto-correction and predictive text in the suggestion bar, text replacement</li>
<li>Emoji view with search and categories</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
LiBoard has no internet permission. Dictionaries, learned words and test results stay on the device. No account, no cloud.

LiBoard is based on HeliBoard (GPL-3.0) and is not affiliated with Apple Inc.
